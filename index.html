Is FUSD a stablecoin?
NO. We are not a stablecoin. We have no peg to USD, no fiat backing, and no guarantee of price stability. Price is determined purely by market supply and demand on decentralized exchanges.
Are you related to Tether (USDT)?
NO. We have no relation to Tether or any other stablecoin project. FUSD is our ticker symbol, but we are a completely separate, community-driven project with no institutional backing whatsoever.
What is the official FUSD contract?
The official FUSD token is live on TRON (TRC-20). Contract: TTAZrVsDeRQgGT3V1jGmpz3gqkdVKM4Ytz. Always verify this address before interacting with the token.
What backs the value of FUSD?
Nothing but community trust. There are no fiat reserves, no banking partners, and no guarantees. The price is determined entirely by what the community is willing to pay on decentralized exchanges. This is a social experiment in decentralized value.
Which networks are supported?
